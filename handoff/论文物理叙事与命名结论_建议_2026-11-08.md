## 2. Summary 重排（核心：先信息、后模型、再结果）

参考草稿（可直接给 C 改，约 250 词）：

> How much can two early positions tell us about where a penalty kick crosses the goal plane? We propagate measurement uncertainty through a flight model with gravity, quadratic drag and a transverse Magnus force, and first derive the information limit of the measurement design. For a fixed start and two observations at 0.05 and 0.10 s, second-order extrapolation amplifies per-axis position noise by Λ(n)=√(L1²+L2²); with arrival times from the launch prior this gives a median extrapolation error of 1.177Λσ, which reproduces the measured kinematic-baseline errors at 2, 5 and 15 mm. A local Bayesian analysis under the physical prior leaves an irreducible median crossing spread of about 0.26 m at 5 mm. An adaptive Bayesian sampler and a physics-informed mixture density network approach this limit: the frozen network has a median error of 0.290 m on an independent 5 mm test, within about 15% of the limit. For the posed case it gives (Y,Z)=(2.62,2.15) m, joint 50%/95% areas 0.177/0.775 m², and a 97.3% conditional in-frame probability; calibrated coverage is 47.6–49.1% and 94.5–95.2%. Tracked penalties and 100 Hz curved-kick data provide case-level checks. The observability ratio of the design crosses unity at about 9 mm tracking noise, and one additional 5 mm position at 0.15 s would lower the bound from 0.26 to 0.10 m: at this noise level a single deterministic forecast is incomplete, and the probability regions are an intrinsic part of the answer.

## 4. 可命名结论（建议最终论文里明确命名 2–3 个）

- 每个命名结论在正文至少出现 2 次（摘要/引言一次，附录/讨论一次）；
- 配一张"一眼看懂"的图（Fig. 5 已可：误差 vs 噪声 + 比值柱状）；

## 5. "怎样寻找可命名的物理结论"（方法论，供后续复用）

1. **找标度律**：误差 ∝ σ、∝ Δt⁻²、∝ n² 之类；无量纲化后看是否只依赖一个组合量（如 Λ(n)）。
2. **找无量纲判据/阈值**：把两个物理量相除（观测噪声加速度 / 先验加速度 = R），令其等于 1 得到临界值（σ*≈9 mm）——比"我们误差 0.29 m"更有物理味。
3. **找不可能性/边界**：信息不足导致的下界、失效边界（高噪声/少帧/自旋未知）比"又一个模型"更打动评委。
4. **用对照实验验证命名结论**：理论下界 vs 实测基线（我们已吻合到 6%），这是"理论不是空话"的证据。
5. **量化 trade-off**：多一帧/降噪/加自旋传感器各能换来多少米（我们：第三帧 0.26→0.10 m）。
6. **命名要短、可复述**：≤4 个英文词，首字母可缩写（如 R(σ)、σ*）。
7. **呈现模板**：名字 → 一句话定义 → 公式 → 一个数字 → 对实际决策的含义 → 图/表。
