# Physical parameter audit

| Model quantity | Evidence and limits |
|---|---|
| Mass 0.43 kg, radius 0.11 m | Compatible with [IFAB Law 2](https://theifab.com/laws/latest/the-ball/): 0.410–0.450 kg and 0.68–0.70 m circumference, equivalent to 0.1082–0.1114 m radius. These are nominal model choices. No mass or radius measurement was supplied for the particular ball. |
| Goal plane 11 m, centre-point rectangle ±3.66 m and 0–2.44 m | [IFAB Law 1](https://theifab.com/laws/latest/the-field-of-play/) gives the penalty mark and 7.32 m × 2.44 m opening. The centre-point scoring rule is the task definition. The exact initial centre (0,0,0.11) m is also a task input. |
| Air density 1.225 kg/m³ and gravity 9.81 m/s² | [Standard-atmosphere constants](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20120003268/downloads/20120003268.pdf?attachment=true) give 1.225 and 9.80665 respectively. Actual weather and local gravity are unknown. |
| Standard drag prior U(0.12, 0.30) | [Asai & Seo 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3657093/) report coefficients about 0.12–0.15 at the critical Reynolds numbers for four balls. [Bray & Kerwin 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12630787/) report 0.25–0.30 from free-kick modelling. [Goff & Carré 2012](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/98035/) discuss drag-crisis dependence. The interval is literature-informed and the uniform density and constant-in-flight drag are modelling assumptions. Wide U(0.10,0.45) is a sensitivity assumption. |
| Lift law and 0.38 cap | [Goff & Carré 2010](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/98053/) and [2012](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/98035/) support spin-dependent lift, and Bray & Kerwin report 0.23–0.29 for their free-kick fits. The exact rational curve and the 0.38 cap were not verified in the Section 1 football sources. |
| Speed U(18,35) m/s; wide U(15,38) | [Kellis & Katis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3786235/) report World Cup ball speeds reaching 32–35 m/s. [Barber & Carré 2010](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/98036/1/J_2010_The%20effect%20of%20surface%20geometry%20on%20soccer%20ball%20trajectories%20%28accepted%20version%29.pdf) report ~24 m/s for curling free kicks. The [Physics World account of Goff & Carré's Beckham analysis](https://physicsworld.com/page/1536/?action=view_event&category=&event_id=576&month=&prevact=list&prevsubact=all&subject=&version=1&year=) reports 36 m/s for that free kick. No penalty-specific endpoints or uniform distribution verified. |
| Elevation 0.5–28° and azimuth ±25°; wide 0.5–35° and ±30° | Bray & Kerwin's 16.5–17.5° is a specific free-kick scenario. It does not validate either penalty angle prior. All four intervals remain assumptions. |
| Spin U(0,120) rad/s, wide U(0,150), isotropic axis | Barber & Carré report ~106 rad/s for curling free kicks. The Physics World account reports ~63 rad/s. [Ronkainen et al.](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/316800112_The_Engineering_of_Sport_7) report discrepancies across measurement methods. No penalty-specific bounds, uniform magnitude, or isotropic spin-axis distribution verified. |
| Robustness and noise | Crosswind 3 m/s, drag slope and spin decay time 10 s are experimental perturbations. No case-specific values were measured. 2.9 mm is the standard deviation of uniform 1 cm rounding error (0.01/√12); independent Gaussian error and the other noise scales are assumptions. [SoccerNet-v3D/ISSIA-3D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10106) document triangulated ball annotations but do not establish millimetre error here. |

The Goff & Carré 2009 launcher settings and full Lees review were not independently verified in a publisher or institutional copy during this audit. The simulator time step and flight cutoff are numerical settings. Cross-sectional area and aerodynamic factor follow from the specified radius, density, and mass.
